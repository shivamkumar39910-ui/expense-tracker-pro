# EXPENSE TRACKER PRO 2.0
## USER AUTOMATION & REAL-WORLD INTELLIGENCE GUIDE

Welcome to Expense Tracker Pro 2.0 Automation! This guide explains how to use the automated intelligence features built in Phase 6 to eliminate manual bookkeeping, discover hidden subscription leaks, visualize cash runway, and receive proactive financial alerts.

---

### 1. Smart SMS & UPI Parser (Paste & Confirm)

#### How It Works:
Whenever you receive an SMS or bank notification on your mobile phone (from HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, or UPI apps like Google Pay, PhonePe, and Paytm):
1. **Copy** the SMS text to your clipboard.
2. Tap the **+** (Quick Add) button at the bottom center of the app.
3. Tap the green **SMS** button in the top right corner.
4. Tap **📋 Paste** or select one of the instant simulation presets (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis).
5. Tap **⚡ Analyze & Structure Transaction**.

#### Safe User Confirmation:
- The parser immediately extracts the **Amount**, **Merchant / Payee**, **Date**, **Account**, and **Suggested Category**.
- It checks whether this transaction has already been recorded in your ledger within the last 2 days. If a duplicate is detected, a **Potential Duplicate Warning** banner will alert you with the date and reference number.
- You can review and adjust any field (change category, edit notes, switch accounts).
- Tap **✅ Confirm & Save** to persist it into your official ledger.
- *Guaranteed Peace of Mind*: The app will never silently record a transaction without your explicit tap.

### 2. Cash-Flow Calendar & Runway

#### Where to Find It:
Go to **Profile & Settings** > **⚡ Automation & Real-World Intelligence** > **Cash-Flow Calendar & Runway**.

#### What You Will See:
1. **Lowest Projected Balance**: Displays the lowest point your liquid balance (Cash + Bank accounts) will reach during the current month.
2. **Runway Runout**: How many days you can safely operate at your current pace before reaching a critical low-balance threshold (Rs. 2,000).
3. **Daily Flow Timeline**:
   - **Past Days**: Displays your actual recorded income and spending.
   - **Today**: Highlighted with your current net position.
   - **Future Days**: Shows upcoming scheduled recurring bills and projected daily discretionary velocity, calculating your estimated end-of-day balance.

### 3. Subscription Intelligence & Leaks Audit

#### Where to Find It:
Go to **Profile & Settings** > **⚡ Automation & Real-World Intelligence** > **Subscription Discovery & Audits**.

#### Powerful Features:
1. **Annualized Outflow**: Automatically calculates your total yearly commitment for all active services (e.g. Rs. 848/month = Rs. 10,176/year).
2. **Duplicate Subscription Warnings**: If you are paying for the same brand multiple times (for example, *Netflix Mobile* and *Netflix Premium*), the engine warns you so you can cancel redundant plans.
3. **Discovered Recurring Patterns**: Scans your 90-day transaction history to find recurring charges (like Spotify, gym memberships, or broadband) that you haven't yet tracked as recurring bills. Tap **+ Track Bill** to monitor them with one touch!

---

### 4. Smart Financial Alerts & Notifications

#### Real-Time Alerts on Home Dashboard:
When an urgent financial situation occurs, an alert banner automatically appears on your **Home** dashboard:
- 🔴 **Budget Overrun Risk**: If your spending velocity indicates you will exceed your monthly budget.
- 🟡 **Upcoming or Overdue Bills**: Alerts you 3 days before a bill is due so you avoid late fees.
- 🟡 **Unusual Category Spending**: Detects when your spending in a category is statistically higher than your historical average.
- 🟡 **Savings Goal Behind Schedule**: Informs you if your contributions need to increase to hit your target date.

#### Dismissing Alerts:
Tap the alert banner to open the **Financial Alerts Modal**. Review the details and tap **Dismiss** when resolved. Alerts are automatically deduplicated within a 24-hour window to protect you from notification fatigue.
